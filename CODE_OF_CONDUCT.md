# Community conduct

Themis welcomes researchers, engineers, students, and practitioners with different disciplines and levels of experience. Participation requires respectful, evidence-oriented collaboration.

Be constructive when challenging a model, result, or claim. Distinguish technical criticism from personal judgment; disclose uncertainty and conflicts of interest; respect privacy; and do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately demean others. Scientific disagreement is welcome. Abuse is not.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe, productive community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact mechanism when public discussion would be inappropriate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
